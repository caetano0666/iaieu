04 · How we work

First we understand. Then we build.

No menu can predict what each person needs. There is a rigorous process for reaching the answer.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We read the person, the business, the context and what already exists.

  2. 02

    Identify

    We separate symptom from problem and find the point that changes direction.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Research, strategy, writing, design and technology become one line of thought.

  4. 04

    Implement

    The idea becomes concrete, functional and ready to use.

  5. 05

    Review

    We challenge the answer with evidence, correct it and document the process.
